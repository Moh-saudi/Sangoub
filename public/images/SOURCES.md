## Government District, New Administrative Capital, Egypt

The four `government-district-*.jpg` files are frames from the publicly hosted video `الحي الحكومي.mp4`:
https://github.com/sasagaming496/New-Capital-of-Egypt/blob/c0373373d8667d91dc2b2dae7514c858ac015948/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D9%8A.mp4

The source page explicitly identifies the footage as the Government District. The footage carries the Administrative Capital for Urban Development (ACUD) mark. The repository uploader is not identified as the photographer and no explicit reuse license was found in that repository. The initial extraction preserved the source mark. The current copies were subsequently replaced by the owner with refreshed crops; this web update uses those supplied files unchanged and adds no synthetic imagery or replacement buildings.

- government-district-gardens.jpg: frame at 00:14 — ministry buildings and landscaped pedestrian axis.
- government-district-ministries.jpg: frame at 00:35 — aerial view of government buildings and streets.
- government-district-skyline.jpg: frame at 00:56 — aerial panorama of ministry buildings with the mosque in the background.
- government-district-night.jpg: frame at 01:24 — government complex lit at night.

Initial frames were exported at 1600×900 with ffmpeg. The owner supplied updated crops in commit e745d7c: gardens 1600×841, ministries 1600×836, skyline 1600×812, and night 1600×819. The web hero uses these four updated files; phone layouts preserve their full intrinsic aspect ratios above the copy. They illustrate the service area; they are not photographs of Capital Talabat's employees or completed deliveries. The older breakfast and coffee photos remain illustrative food images in the menu overview, not claims about particular local restaurants.
